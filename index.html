About Us

Welcome to Shree B.P. Foods.

For more than 30+ years, Shree B.P. Foods has been serving customers with premium quality rice in Motihari and across Bihar. We are an authorized dealer of Zeeba Basmati Rice and Ripuraj Premium Rice.

Shree B.P. Foods is famous for premium quality rice in Bihar. It deals in ZEEBA BASMATI RICE (a Government-recognized 5-Star company). As a top-selling Basmati rice brand worldwide, its varieties like Mini Mongra, Mongra, Super Mongra, Dubar, Tibar, Premium, and Classic Basmati rice have been spreading aroma in Motihari and across Bihar for over 10 years.

We also offer RIPURAJ Premium Basmati, as well as kheer and jeera rice options like Sonashakti, Mahashakti, Zayka, and Sonachoor. RIPURAJ continues to lead the market in Jeera rice across India.

Our mission is to deliver fresh, hygienic, and premium quality rice at competitive prices with complete customer satisfaction. We proudly serve retailers, wholesalers, restaurants, hotels, and households.

These rice varieties are available 365 days a year at our shop. Heartly welcome to my shop, thank you!
